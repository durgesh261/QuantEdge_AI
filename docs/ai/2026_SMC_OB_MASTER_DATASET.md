## 3. Strict Separation of Causal AI Features vs Post-Decision Outcomes

To ensure 100% mathematical purity and zero data leakage in downstream AI modeling:
1. **Causal-at-Decision Features (`feat_*`)**: Calculated strictly from candles, pivots, and breaks with `index <= decision_bar_index`. The cutoff timestamp is explicitly recorded in `feature_cutoff_timestamp`.
2. **Post-Decision Lifecycle Fields**: Columns such as `invalidation_status`, `first_touch_occurred`, `first_touch_result`, `realized_r`, `mfe_r`, `mae_r` are post-decision forward outcomes provided strictly for audit and target labeling. They **MUST NEVER** be passed as input features into an AI model.
